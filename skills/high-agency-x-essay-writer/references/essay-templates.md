# Essay Templates

Use these templates as scaffolding. Adapt the section names to the topic.

## Long-Form X Article

```md
# [Hard-truth or transformation title]

[Opening paragraph that states the tension, mistake, or consequence.]

[Second paragraph that sharpens the claim and raises the stakes.]

## The real problem

[Explain why the common framing is incomplete or wrong.]

## What is actually happening

[Reveal the deeper mechanism: identity, attention, environment, incentives, leverage, or systems.]

## How to fix it

### 1. [First principle]

[Short explanation.]

### 2. [Second principle]

[Short explanation.]

### 3. [Third principle]

[Short explanation.]

## The protocol

- [Action 1]
- [Action 2]
- [Action 3]

## Final takeaway

[Finish with a line that compresses the whole thesis into one practical principle.]
```

## Newsletter-Style Essay

```md
# [High-agency title]

[A strong opening with a tension or hard truth.]

[A paragraph that reframes the problem.]

## Why most people stay stuck

[Explain the trap.]

## The shift that changes everything

[Introduce the central principle.]

## Build the right system

- [Behavioral shift]
- [Environmental shift]
- [Skill or leverage shift]

## What to do next

[Give a short protocol, challenge, or rule set.]
```

## Chinese Essay

[第二段补上误区，说明大多数人为什么会判断错。]

## 真正的问题不在表面

[短解释。]

[短解释。]

## Title Patterns

Use these patterns loosely. Do not clone any specific creator's headline.

- `The Real Reason You Still Can't [Desired Outcome]`
- `Most People Will Never [Desired Outcome]. Here's Why.`
- `How to Build a Life That Makes [Desired Outcome] Inevitable`
- `The Skill That Quietly Changes Everything`
- `Why [Common Advice] Keeps You Weak`
- `Stop Chasing [Wrong Variable]. Build [Better Variable]`
- `你迟迟做不成 [目标]，问题可能根本不在 [表面原因]`
- `大多数人卡住，不是因为不努力，而是因为 [更深原因]`
- `别再追 [错误变量] 了，真正该建立的是 [正确变量]`
- `真正拉开差距的，不是 [常见认知]，而是 [关键能力]`

## Rewrite Sequence

When the user gives an existing draft:

1. Tighten the title into a stronger promise or confrontation.
2. Replace the opening with a sharper tension.
3. Merge repetitive paragraphs.
4. Organize the body into 3-7 distinct claims.
5. End with a practical operating principle.
