# Style Signals

Use this file when the goal is to produce a high-agency, contrarian, self-improvement essay inspired by public creator article patterns without copying any living author's voice.

## Observed Structural Traits in Dan Koe X Articles

- Titles are big promises, hard truths, or total reframes.
- Openings create immediate tension: the reader is doing something wrong, optimizing the wrong variable, or underestimating a deeper principle.
- Paragraphs are short and punchy. Many key lines stand alone.
- Essays often move from surface problem to deeper cause, then into a practical model or method.
- Ideas combine psychology, behavior change, leverage, systems, identity, and long-term personal outcomes.
- The tone is assertive and reflective at the same time.
- Conclusions usually condense into a protocol, checklist, or worldview shift.

## High-Level Traits to Reuse Safely

- Contrarian hook
- Strong thesis early
- System-level reasoning
- Emphasis on agency, attention, discipline, skill, leverage, and environment
- Scannable sections with idea-dense lines
- Actionable ending

## Traits to Avoid Copying

- Signature slogans or catchphrases
- Distinctive metaphors repeated across the same creator's catalog
- Title formulas that map too closely to specific known articles
- Any sentence that feels recognizable as a near-paraphrase

## Writing Moves to Prefer

- "The real problem is not X. It is Y."
- "Most people stay stuck because..."
- "You do not need more information. You need a better operating system."
- "The point is not to feel motivated. The point is to make the right behavior inevitable."
- "Once you understand this, the rest becomes obvious."

## Writing Moves to Avoid

- Empty motivational language
- Spiritual vagueness without practical consequence
- Overly polished corporate tone
- Long explanatory paragraphs with weak progression
- Generic listicles with no argument spine

## Content Priorities

1. Sharp thesis
2. Reader tension
3. Deeper reframe
4. Structured reasoning
5. Actionable conclusion

If the choice is between sounding profound and being useful, choose useful.

## Chinese Adaptation Layer

Use this when the output should be Chinese rather than translated English.

- Favor judgment-first Chinese phrasing over motivational build-up.
- Make transitions more explicit than in English when needed: "先说结论", "问题在这里", "更深一层看", "落到行动上".
- Keep paragraphs short, but allow slightly fuller explanation than the English version when it improves clarity.
- Replace abstract self-optimization language with concrete Chinese frames: attention allocation, execution friction, habit environment, long-term compounding.
- Prefer clean, hard-edged endings over lyrical flourishes.

### Chinese Opening Moves

- `先说结论：你卡住，不是因为不够努力，而是努力用错了方向。`
- `很多人以为问题在执行力，其实问题更早，在判断。`
- `真正把人拉开差距的，不是信息量，而是能不能把自己放进正确的系统里。`

### Chinese Ending Moves

- `所以结论很简单：不要继续优化表面的勤奋，去重建底层的系统。`
- `一旦这件事想清楚，后面的动作反而不复杂。`
- `你不需要再给自己打气。你需要的是一个让正确行为更容易发生的环境。`
