# Image Planning

Use this file when deciding how many images the article should have.

## Baseline

For most articles:

- 1 thumbnail
- 1-2 section images

## When to add section images

Add a section image when:

- the section introduces a new conceptual frame
- the section would benefit from a diagrammatic visual
- the article is long and needs rhythm relief

## When not to add section images

Do not add one when:

- the section is short
- the visual would repeat the paragraph literally
- the article is already dense with screenshots or charts

## Prompt Style

Prompts should specify:

- subject
- composition
- visual metaphor
- palette
- rendering feel
- "no text" unless text is explicitly desired
